Event-Driven Architecture Simulator

Build a small event-driven system out of producers, topics/queues and consumers, wire them together, then press play. Tune throughput, processing latency and failure rates — or simulate a consumer crash — and watch backlogs, drops and failures play out in real time.

Add a producer, a queue and a consumer, then drag from a node's right-edge dot to another node to connect them.

How to use it — drag nodes to rearrange the canvas. Drag from the small dot on a node's right edge onto another node to wire them up (producers → queues, queues → consumers). Click any node to open its settings: message rate, processing latency, failure chance, queue capacity, or a "simulate crash" switch for consumers. Press play and watch the queue fill bars, the in-flight message dots and the live counters react as you change the dials.
