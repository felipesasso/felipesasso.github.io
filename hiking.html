Hiker's Survival Guide

🚌

Public Transport

Most trails are accessible via bus lines leaving from the main terminals (TITRI or TILAG). Use the 'Floripa no Ponto' app for live schedules.

🚤

The "Escape" Boat

For Costa da Lagoa and similar lakeside trails, look for the official boat transport (Cooperbarco) if you prefer not to walk back.

🐍

Trail Safety

Pit vipers (jararacas) are well-camouflaged in the forest leaf litter. Stick to the paths and avoid thick brush in flip-flops.

Essential Kit

  • Water (minimum 1L-2L)
  • Insect repellent (essential for forest trails)
  • Sturdy footwear with good grip
  • Flashlight (for sunset hikes)
